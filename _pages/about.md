I build machine-learning models that turn routinely collected hospital data into **perioperative decision support** that clinicians can read and trust. Since October 2026 I am **Maître d'enseignement et de recherche (Senior Lecturer and Researcher)** at the **University of Fribourg**, and I continue as **Advanced Postdoctoral Researcher** in the Department of Visceral Surgery and Medicine at **Inselspital, Bern University Hospital and University of Bern**, in the group of Prof. Guido Beldi. I am also a member of the Department of Digital Medicine in Bern and of OHDSI Switzerland.

My work follows a patient from the operating room to the ward. I develop **explainable risk models** for postoperative infection ([JAMIA 2025](https://doi.org/10.1093/jamia/ocaf145)), read risk from **intraoperative vital-sign dynamics** ([npj Digital Medicine 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41746-026-02707-1)), and use **large language models** to align clinical terms across languages ([Scientific Reports 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-34778-7)). I **audit models for equity** across patient subgroups before deployment, with the open-source [isitfair](https://github.com/HugoGuillen/isitfair) library (JAMIA 2026, in press). I also lead the AI and methods component of a University of Bern project on **federated learning and synthetic data** across Swiss hospitals. More on the [research](/research/) and [software](/software/) pages.

I came to medicine from computer science. I hold a **PhD in Computer Science** from CICESE, Mexico (2018), and I was a **Marie Skłodowska-Curie Fellow** at University College Dublin (2021–2023), after a postdoc in Bern on the computational genomics of long noncoding RNA. That work appeared in *Nature Communications*, *Cell Genomics*, *Nucleic Acids Research* and *Genome Research*. My full [CV](/cv/) lists teaching, talks and grants.
