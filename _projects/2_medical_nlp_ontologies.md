Clinical data is fragmented across languages, terminologies, and coding systems. This line of work uses **large-language-model-augmented semantic embeddings** to map medical-procedure terms across languages and standard ontologies, supporting interoperability of electronic-health-record data for downstream research and clinical decision support.

**Selected publications**

- Guillen-Ramirez, H. *et al.* **LLM-augmented semantic embeddings enable cross-lingual mapping of medical procedure terms.** *Scientific Reports* (2026). [doi:10.1038/s41598-025-34778-7](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-34778-7)
